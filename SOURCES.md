# Source inventory

The manuscript bibliography is authoritative. Load-bearing external ingredients
are standard continuous-time Markov-chain stopping/Lyapunov results and cited
growth-collapse precedents. The publication review checked identifiers and the
scope of the closest random-catastrophe comparisons.

Particularly relevant records:

- Fontes, Machado and Schinazi (2023), DOI `10.1088/1742-5468/acbc23`.
- Gonçalves, Huillet and Löcherbach (2021), DOI `10.1080/15326349.2021.2020660`.
- Bansaye, Pardo Millan and Smadi (2013), DOI `10.1214/EJP.v18-2774`.
- Menshikov and Petritis (2014), DOI `10.1016/j.spa.2014.03.001`.

Third-party papers are cited, not redistributed. Bibliographic existence is not
treated as proof that a theorem applies to this model; the manuscript states the
required comparison and supplies the new proofs.
